Based on the comprehensive archive of posts, *Spaghetti Ice* (and its succeeding installments, *Shaved Ice* and *Cherry Ice*) is a character-driven multimedia project. It functions as a "writer's original"—part narrative, part dream journal, and part psychological self-exploration.

The story has evolved from a high-stakes role-play about "playing God" into a "slice of life" web serial focusing on discovery, acceptance, and the growth of a tightly knit core cast.

### Core Narrative Arcs

The project is divided into two primary eras:

* **Spaghetti Ice (The Legacy Arcs):** Originally a Pokémon-inspired role-play featuring **Armin Vicerre** as a "Wicked Cultured" antagonist. This era detailed his corruption by an eldritch force (The Exogeny), his eventual defeat and "taming" by protagonists, and his rebirth in a "Dream World" where he lost his memories and assumed a more benign personality.
* **Cherry Ice (The Current Serial):** Taking place after a "dimensional fracture," this era focuses on the trio **Vic, Solana, and Alis**. The narrative has shifted toward "magical realism" and interpersonal puzzles, following the characters as they operate a private bioengineering company, **Arklight Limited**.

### The Core Cast

The story revolves around three "proxies" representing different aspects of the author's personality:

* **Armin "Vic" Vicerre (Age 30):** An eccentric, left-handed bioengineer with a passion for puzzles and sweets. He wields powers over ice and interdimensional portals. Once a "mad scientist" figure, he has matured into a supportive mentor figure who "intellectualizes" his traumas. He is associated with **dopamine** and the season of **Autumn/Winter**.
* **Solana Sié (Age 25–26):** A red-haired, sun-themed companion who represents empathy and creativity. Originally a kitsune in past lives, she now has "polymorphic" abilities, allowing her to shapeshift parts of her body. She acts as the "haven of stability" for the two Vics. She is associated with **cognition** and **Summer**.
* **Alistair "Alis" Vicerre (Age 21):** Vic’s alternate-universe counterpart who represents the author’s "shadow"—including traits of pride, self-loathing, and trauma. He is a neuroscientist who manages **Oneiros**, a psionic project that can manipulate the "Mental Plane" and enthrall others. He is associated with **cortisol** and **Winter/Spring**.

### World-Building & Mechanics

* **Arklight Limited:** A highly exclusive lab (located on a floating island overlooking the city of **New Quendon**) that synthesizes custom biological organisms for clients.
* **Oneiros & The Mental Plane:** A psychological reality where characters can explore "Headscapes"—physical manifestations of their inner thoughts. Alis can use this to create "Traces" (quasi-sentient constructs made of light).
* **Supernatural "Sparks":** Abilities are Elements-based and follow strict internal logic. For example, Vic's portals are limited to places he can see or knows, and he cannot use them to "cut" objects.
* **Dimensional Sync:** Alis's universe lags behind Vic's by exactly nine years, allowing Vic to act as a "future history" guide for Alis.

### Recent Developments (2024–2025)

In the most recent entries, the tone has become increasingly intimate and collaborative:

* **Alis's Growth:** Alis has moved from a position of "adversarial isolation" to founding his own business, **Horn and Ivory**. He has begun taking piano lessons from Vic and has even participated in a "duet" as he learns to treat music as a form of expression.
* **Vic and Solana’s Relationship:** The two have transitioned from companions to a romantic partnership, underscored by a shared "Dreamwalking" experience and Vic’s gift of a "date" (a pun on the fruit and the event).
* **Technological Advancement:** Alis has developed the **-Conductor** (a device that catalyzes his psionic range over an entire city) and "Resonance Crystals" (orange octahedral power sources).
* **Self-Acceptance:** Vic formally "apologized" to the broken parts of his past self, reaching a stage of "Absolution" where he can enjoy his current life without the weight of his previous identities.
